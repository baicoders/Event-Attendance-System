# Classroom attendance: teacher guide

Open the school's attendance website on your phone with an internet connection. Choose **Class** when signing in. You use the deployed website; there is nothing to install or run.

## First visit

Apply for Teacher Access with your school details. Pending means an administrator has not reviewed it yet. Rejected applications show the reason and a way to correct and resubmit. After approval, you manage classes and attendance independently.

The guided setup has three chapters: **Meet Classroom**, **Set up your class**, and **Try attendance**. You can skip the introduction and practice. Choose a subject and academic term, then create your class after choosing sections, or select an existing class you own. Use **Save and exit** to keep a draft before creation or save valid edits afterward; it does not confirm a review checkpoint. Invalid entries stay visible so you can correct them. Once created, subject and term remain fixed. You may choose multiple sections for one subject. Review the automatically included students, add irregular students, and exclude students who do not take this subject. These exceptions stay saved for the class. Adding an irregular student does not change their official section. You can fix setup later from Manage Roster.

Set optional schedule details and a late grace period. Times use Asia/Manila. A scan at the threshold is Present; later scans are Late. The tutorial is a demonstration and creates no attendance. The final screen, **Your class is ready**, shows your saved class. **Open my classes** completes setup. **Start attendance** opens a review screen; you must explicitly confirm before a real meeting begins. Setup and practice never create attendance. An empty class can be saved, but include at least one student before confirming its roster.

## Each meeting

Open **My Classes → your class → Start Attendance**. Check the meeting start time and roster preview, then explicitly start. Opening the class page does not mark anyone absent. Continue an existing active session instead of starting another.

Tap Open Camera and allow camera access. Scan students' **existing** QR codes yourself. Wait for Saved before counting the mark as recorded. A repeated scan reports already marked and never toggles attendance. Unknown/retired codes need manual lookup or an administrator's identity investigation; do not guess whose code it is.

A valid student outside the roster prompts a choice: include this meeting only, add permanently, or cancel. Confirm whether to include/mark them in the current meeting. Permanent roster changes affect future meetings; older snapshots stay intact.

## Manual attendance and connection problems

Switch to **Manual attendance** at any time. An active meeting opens one unmarked student at a time, with their name, USN/LRN and section. Choose **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excuse**. Excuse notes are optional. The card stays open while saving, then advances after confirmation. Progress and the last confirmed result stay visible. Use **Review mark** to correct the last saved student with a reason.

Choose **Roster** to search all students, filter any status, inspect excluded students and review or correct marks. Roster pages hold 100 students. Switching between Focus and Roster preserves your roster preferences; finalized and canceled meetings open roster review. A search with no unmarked matches does not mean everyone has been marked.

Saving means the request is in progress; Saved means the server acknowledged it. If a save is unconfirmed, use **Check same save / Retry**. Reload checks pending operations in their original order before another student can be marked. Recovery uses the original operation so a retry cannot duplicate a mark or start. A failed roster refresh does not undo a confirmed save; refresh the roster to continue. Keep the tab open until resolved. Closing the tab or signing out removes local retry details and pending notes, but does not remove records already saved on the server. Ask an administrator to inspect history if the result remains unclear.

Internet is required. There is no offline queue and a disconnected screen must not claim success. If camera permission is denied, allow it in your browser's site settings or use Manual. Close other apps using the camera. If the reader cannot load, check the connection and reload after resolving pending saves. Manual also requires a server acknowledgment.

## Finish and correct

Choose **Review & finalize** when you are ready. Finishing the focused cards never finalizes automatically. Remaining Unmarked students become Absent together. Use **Session options** to adjust the late policy or cancel a mistaken meeting; canceled meetings do not generate absences and are excluded from reports. A finalized attendance correction requires a reason and stays in immutable history. Concurrent changes show a conflict: refresh and review the latest value before retrying.

## Student information and reports

Students is the shared official directory. You may correct permitted individual fields with a reason; another teacher's recent edit is protected by version checks. Use **Verify student details** to check the name, USN/LRN, and section against school records, then choose **Verify details** or **Flag for correction**. Verification is optional for attendance. Changes to student details need another check. **Add a note** reveals an optional new note; saved notes remain visible. Open **Change history** when you need the recorded reasons and readable comparisons under **View changes**. Identifier corrections require a deliberate keep-safe-alias or retire/reprint choice. Never give an old printed code to another student. Bulk imports, merging and deletion are administrator tasks.

Reports shows your classes' historical meetings in **Class**, **Student**, **Day** or **Meeting** views. The headline cards show attendance rate, meetings held, attended and absences. Search the student table by historical name, USN or LRN; this filters the table while the headline metrics and charts keep the full report scope. **Export → All students in report** and **Print** include the full prepared report, independent of table search/page. Calculation details are available in **How this report is calculated**. Late counts as attended; Excused is separate and excluded from the attendance-rate denominator. No eligible meetings means N/A. Newly enrolled students are not absent for meetings before they joined. If a correction changes a prepared export, refresh the report before downloading/printing. General reports omit excuse notes. You cannot view another teacher's private attendance merely because you can find the student.

## Common questions

- **Can an administrator start my meeting?** No. Only the approved, enabled teacher who owns the class may start. Administrators can view existing sessions and make audited assistance/corrections.
- **Must an administrator approve my class or irregular students?** No. Teacher application is the only new approval.
- **Must I rebuild the roster tomorrow?** No. Open the saved class and start the next meeting.
- **Can I hold two meetings on one day?** Yes. Finalize/cancel the current meeting first, then explicitly start the next one.
- **Can I also organize Events?** Yes, if you separately hold Events access. Use the workspace switcher. Class suspension does not remove a separate Events grant.
- **I cannot see Class anymore.** Check access status and contact the administrator. Repeated login or changing a workspace preference does not grant access.


## Finding your next Classroom task

**Dashboard** is the overview: your current class count, active meetings, meetings today in Asia/Manila, attendance progress, recent classes and recent meeting histories. Today includes active and finalized meetings, counts each meeting separately and leaves out canceled meetings. Choose a term to narrow the overview. Setup guidance disappears after setup is complete; Teacher guide & help remains in Settings.

**My Classes** manages saved classes. Search, choose an academic term or include archived classes; these choices and the current page can be shared through the URL. A card has one primary attendance action and an Open class action. Open a class for its Overview, Roster and Settings pages. Class options also provides reports. Archived classes retain their history and cannot start a new meeting.

**Attendance** has two views. Active meetings lets you resume an existing meeting. Start a meeting shows nonarchived classes that have no active meeting. Switching views keeps the term and search. Review the saved roster and meeting time before confirming Start attendance. Administrators view existing sessions and assist teachers; the teacher starts the meeting.

**Class setup and roster** use grouped sections. Save class details, select official student sections and then review the saved roster. Saved roster review is separate from pending section choices and individual additions/exclusions. Review the change summary and save before starting attendance. A recoverable error retains your selections. If another person changes the roster or settings, reload the saved version and review your retained draft before saving again.

**Students** shows official section and verification state beside each labeled USN/LRN. Search runs on the server across the directory and keeps 100 students per page. Open a student to review official information, verify it, or make a correction with a reason. Technical record versions and raw change data are available in disclosures. Student attendance links respect your authorized class history.

**Settings** groups account details/password, classroom setup/help and workspace access. Administrators also have direct navigation to Teachers, Classes, Academic catalog, Activity and Settings, including on a phone. Account menus retain workspace switching.

Attendance results are confirmed only when the server responds. If a save is uncertain, use the same-save retry and keep the pending entries; do not start a replacement meeting. Focus, scanner/manual switching, finalization and historical report/CSV/print behavior continue to use the existing saved meeting snapshots.


**Build attendance report** starts with Class, Student, Day, and Meeting choices. Class and Meeting need a class; Student and Day can narrow to a class or include all authorized classes. Date ranges for Class and Student are optional. Review the selection summary, then choose **Prepare report**. Student links resolve the selected student's name and labeled identifiers; retry or change the selection if the record is unavailable.

**Settings** groups Account and password, Teacher guide & help, and Events workspace access in one panel. Account dialogs keep your current Classroom page and filters behind them. Close or Back returns there. Account information is read-only; password changes apply to both workspaces. Wait for a pending submission to finish before closing. Direct account links use the same dialog and a permission-aware Return to workspace action.

## Teacher guide and help

Open **Settings → Teacher guide & help** (`/class/help`). Bookmark individual topics: `#setup`, `#rosters`, `#starting`, `#scanning`, `#manual`, `#finalizing`, `#reports`, and `#students`. Old `/class/onboarding?help=1` bookmarks redirect to the guide.

Labeled Help buttons beside roster management, scanning, manual attendance, finalization and report selection open short lessons in a sheet. Your task stays open and focus returns when you close the sheet. The scanner pauses while its help is open. Help opens only when requested.

Replay the introduction or practice from the guide at any time. Replay never changes completion, creates classes, or saves attendance. The rehearsal uses fictional Alex, Bea and Cai: simulate a scan, mark someone manually, then see Unmarked become Absent when you finalize practice. The optional practice camera is a preview only; it never decodes cards. Unfinished setup has a **Continue setup** invitation; completed teachers retain their summary and access to classes.

Wait for save confirmation before leaving. If a connection or acknowledgement is lost, use **Retry same save**. Do not open another creation attempt: reloading reconciles saved progress first, so a class already created is offered for review.
